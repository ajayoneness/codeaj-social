# Content plan (summary of the strategy doc)

Brand: CodeAj Marketplace (projects.codeaj.com) — 500+ ready AI/ML, web, mobile, blockchain,
cybersecurity projects for BCA/MCA/BTech/BSc IT students, plus live Learning & Mentorship
(project setup + line-by-line code walkthrough + viva prep over Google Meet/AnyDesk).
Founder and face: Ajay Pandit (linkedin.com/in/codeaj). Google: 5.0 stars, 48 reviews.

Positioning: "AI can write your code. Who explains it in the viva?" Competitors only shout
"1000+ projects with source code"; we win on trust, real explanation and mentorship.

Audience is wider than final-year buyers: CS/IT students and young developers in India and abroad.
All content in English. Engagement formats: coding quizzes (guess the output), Git and terminal tips,
interview/DSA questions, AI tools for developers, dev memes. At most 1 in 3 posts is about final-year projects.

Mix: 80% value / relatable (ideas, viva prep, student life, behind the scenes), 20% direct offer.
Goal metric: DM sends and saves (Instagram's strongest signals), then comments on CTA keywords.

Pillars:
1. Project ideas & trends (carousel) — "7 AI projects your guide will approve in 2026"
2. Viva & placement prep (carousel) — questions + model answers, "explain your project in 60 seconds"
3. Student life (reel) — guide vs student, 2 AM errors, the groupmate who only makes the PPT
4. Project demos & problem stories (reel) — QuishGuard (QR scams), VeriChain (fake certificates),
   IntrusionIQ, RailGrid AI, JobLens, road hazard detection
5. Behind the scenes & trust (reel) — how a mentorship session works, Google rating, review quotes

Tech news: breaking developer/AI news replaces the day's theme when it is big (max 3 a week),
and every Friday is a "This week in tech" carousel. Only verified sources, written in our own words.

Other things to feature occasionally: HACKLUME hackathon (certificates, coupons, cash prize),
quiz.codeaj.com (earn points → discount coupons), Roast My Resume, AI Project Idea Finder.

Hooks that work: start with a fear or a question, never with the logo.
Captions: English, short, end with a question + CTA keyword. 4–6 specific hashtags.
